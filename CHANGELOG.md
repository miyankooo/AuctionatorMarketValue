# Auctionator Market Value

## Unreleased

- Show Similar Items is now a setting, on by default, instead of a checkbox under the bag panel that covered the gold display.
- Similar auctions read Similar in the Owned column and show their item level in the item's quality colour.
- A click on a similar auction now undercuts its price, like every other row.
- The Stat Filter applies each change at once. Clear All inside the dialog replaces the Apply and Reset Filter buttons.
- The Filter button shows how many stats are active and keeps its width.
- Tooltips show two rows: 14-day average and Last price vs average.
- Sale Scan greys out while an item sits in the sale slot, and its tooltip says why.
- The stat parser reads the old "magical resistances of your spell targets" wording as Spell Piercing.
- The Similar Items range settings grey out while Show Similar Items is off.
- Opening the settings in combat waits until combat ends.
- Removed the TradeSkillMaster rows and the Require Both Values setting, since TradeSkillMaster has no WoW Forever build.
- Removed the login guide, the Full Scan buttons and the options button on Auctionator's tab. Auctionator's own Full Scan does the same job.
